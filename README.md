🌍 1. About PriceHunter AI

PriceHunter AI is an intelligent, AI-powered price discovery and comparison platform designed to transform how people search for products, compare prices, analyze markets, and make purchasing decisions.

The platform aims to bring product information, seller offers, price history, market analysis, and intelligent recommendations together in one unified ecosystem.

Starting in Iran, PriceHunter AI has a long-term vision of expanding into international markets and becoming a trusted global platform for intelligent price discovery.

Our goal is to make shopping smarter, pricing more transparent, and market information more accessible to everyone.

🎯 2. Our Mission

Our mission is to use technology and artificial intelligence to create a more transparent, competitive, and accessible marketplace.

We aim to:

* Help people find the best available prices.
* Reduce unnecessary purchasing expenses.
* Improve price transparency.
* Support fair competition between sellers.
* Help businesses understand market prices.
* Provide reliable product and pricing information.
* Make intelligent shopping accessible to everyone.
* Help users make informed purchasing decisions.
